6 · Human clarification and authority

Filter generated reviews, then resolve findings

Review owner

Confirms one accountable assignee.

Suggest from configured responsibility or reliable evidence — not simply the message author.

Accountable person

Chooses a source-based answer, or selects Other and explains.

Record the answer, actor, date and supporting evidence. The finding stays open until confirmation.

Decision owner

Checks the response and explicitly confirms.

Only an authorized project / decision owner can resolve it.

Nobody knows?

Leave the finding unresolved. Show its impact and next action.

Unknown owner → review owner routes it. Unknown answer → no “resolved” status or confirmed memory.

Source edited or deleted?

Keep the original evidence history and flag affected reports as stale.

Re-review before confirming or exporting from outdated evidence. New text is not automatically authoritative.

7 · Report, export and the next review

One evidence-linked report and a versioned decision record

Report + consolidated document

Goal + analyzed sources + failures + findings + exact passages + impact + owners + answers + next steps + limitations.

Filter by project, timeline, source and status. Export the report. A rebuilt document uses confirmed answers and marks unresolved gaps. Word and Markdown exports retain confirmed decisions, open issues and relevant original evidence.

Confirmed project memory

Append the decision, confirming person, time, supporting evidence and any superseded decision.

Keep previous versions. On the next review, use only this project’s authorized memory — after the routing gate.

Visible readiness rules

Ready: complete usable evidence; no open findings.
Ready with actions: low / medium open findings.
Needs clarification: high open findings, incomplete / stale evidence or failed analysis.

No unexplained numerical score. Status supports human judgment; it does not approve the project.
